The proclamation

Six weeks have passed. Roy Fontenelle is 6–0, a statistic so offensive to the laws of nature that the Oracle has ordered the kingdom's scholars to recount the wins using their fingers. But this week the undefeated booger-bather faces Nathan Glueck, the old tyrant. Both cannot win. Unfortunately, one of them must.

Chapter VII: The Mucus Schism

At dawn, the gates of Shania's palace swung open and revealed a terrible sight: Roy Fontenelle, undefeated and inexplicably smug, paddling through a vast green ocean of sacred boogers. His cries echoed through the valley. “I'M SIX AND ZERO!” he shouted, as if the slime cared.

High above, Eimak and Shania Twain laughed until the stained-glass windows trembled. The Oracle lifted his scepter and announced: “Six wins do not make a king. They merely make the eventual humiliation more expensive.”

But across the valley, Nathan Glueck gathered the remnants of his fallen court. His brother Sawyer had quietly amassed an army of turtles. Chris Polk's Nation of Domination marched under a banner with one too many words. Jacob's demon worshippers muttered that their allegiance was to darkness, not to Nathan, and certainly not to Roy.

Thus the rebellion split into two factions: the Order of the Sacred Booger, loyal to Eimak's prophecy, and the House of the Last Champion, clinging to Nathan's fading crown. Between them drifted Michael Landry, the shapeless Goop Boi, absorbing discarded identities and whispering bad waiver advice to anyone desperate enough to listen.

And Shania, still laughing, spoke the words that would shake the realm: “This week, one of the villains wins. But when the playoffs come, the throne itself shall answer for its crimes.”
