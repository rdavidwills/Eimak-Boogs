The Goopsgiving prophecy

As Eimak and Shania tore into the sacred turkey leg, the banquet hall fell silent. Nathan’s throne began to wobble. Roy stood to deliver a speech about his ten victories, but the assembled realm asked to see his championship ring. He sat back down.

Then the gravy boat began to speak. Michael Landry, now a gelatinous entity of uncertain allegiance, oozed across the table toward Jacob. The Demon Worshiper whispered an ancient blessing. Jeff and Lucy mistook the creature for a family mascot.

Shania raised the turkey bone. “The revolution is not finished.” Eimak pointed toward Nathan’s throne. The playoffs approach, and the crown shall be contested. Until then, may your flex score more than your uncle’s blood pressure.
