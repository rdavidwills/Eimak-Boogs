📜 Chronicle V: The Goop Covenant

At midnight, the Temple of Rivieris began to smell of lavender, brimstone, and an aggressively expensive hair salon.

Jacob Thomas arrived at the sacred gates, undefeated at 4–0, wearing a velvet cape that trailed seventeen feet behind him. His boots sparkled. His fingernails were lacquered the color of a haunted disco ball. He had not walked here. He had glided, borne upon a carpet of sentient pink mucus.

“You,” Eimak croaked, raising his ancient booger staff. “I knew your success was unnatural.”

Jacob smiled. Behind him, a vast translucent figure rose from the ground: Michael Landry's Goop Boi, a former man who had shed every stable identity and become an enormous, glittering apparition of uncertain allegiance. A thousand tiny mouths opened across its surface and began singing a cursed show tune in perfect harmony.

“Behold,” Jacob announced, flourishing a jeweled wand, “the Grand Duchess of the Unspecified Self. The Viscount of Viscosity. My fabulous and gelatinous benefactor.”

Shania Twain stepped out of the purple mist beside Jacob, carrying a blazing sword and the expression of someone deeply disappointed in the local fantasy football community.

Then Sawyer emerged from the opposite archway, glasses shining beneath a turtle-shell helmet. He struck the ground with his shield. Eimak stood beside him, beard dripping with green fire.

“Your reign ends here, Demon Worshiper,” Sawyer declared.

Jacob snapped his fingers. The Goop Boi launched a tidal wave of iridescent sludge across the courtyard. It struck the walls and spelled out the words FOUR AND OH, DARLING in glittering script.

At that exact moment, Maggie entered carrying an ordinary football. “Does anybody want to actually play the games?”

The battlefield fell silent. Jacob's cape sagged. Somewhere in the distance, Roy began explaining why his undefeated season was historically significant despite his empty trophy shelf. Nobody listened.

Meanwhile, deep beneath the castle, Nathan polished his old championship crown. Above him the revolution had split in two, and each faction was preparing a different ending for his reign.

THE ORDER OF THE SEVERED CROWN

Let Nathan survive. Let him return to the playoffs believing he is untouchable. The final reckoning must come when the crown is heaviest.

THE BOOGER LIBERATION FRONT

No patience. No ceremony. No more Nathan victories. And now they suspect Jacob's Goop Covenant is a second tyranny rising behind the first.

As the sun rose, Eimak saw the future divided into three roads: Nathan's collapsing monarchy, Jacob's flamboyant slime empire, and Sawyer's turtle-backed resistance. Over them all hovered the Goop Boi, humming, shimmering, and changing shape whenever someone tried to describe it.

The Oracle had predicted a revolution. He had not predicted that it would arrive wearing sequins.
