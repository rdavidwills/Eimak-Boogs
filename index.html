The Holy Realm of Shania Twain's Boogers

WEEK 11
THE GROUNDED FLEET

Shania and Dark Lord Eimak survey the wreckage. The Oracle has spoken.

The planes have been grounded. The engines are dead. Yet Roy's 9–1 ego remains airborne, despite a projection barely higher than a 4–6 opponent. As the postseason approaches, the Holy Realm must decide which pretenders deserve a runway and which belong in the baggage carousel of shame.

The Six Prophecies

Poo Poo Magoo vs. Booger Eaters

Both 4–6 · ESPN 93.9–97.7

Oracle's pick: Booger Eaters. This is less a clash of titans than two luggage carts colliding on a deserted runway. Jayden Daniels gives Lucy a legitimate ceiling, but Booger Eaters have a narrow edge with Javonte Williams and the explosive possibilities of Parker Washington. Aaron Rodgers is not the comforting presence a playoff hopeful dreams of, so the Oracle calls a nervous, ugly victory.

Serving Kuentz vs. Carl MARXDONALD'S

Roy 9–1 · Chris 4–6 · ESPN 118.2–117.5

Oracle's pick: Carl MARXDONALD'S — upset. Roy has spent ten weeks parading around like a decorated general, yet a 4–6 team has him sweating through his ceremonial trousers. Josh Allen, Jahmyr Gibbs and Travis Kelce are formidable; Joe Burrow and Ja'Marr Chase offer Chris a spectacular counterattack. One big Bengals afternoon could bring Roy's imperial aircraft down without a single warning light.

Nation of Of Domination vs. Show Me The Turtles

Chris Polk 4–6 · Sawyer 7–3 · ESPN 113.2–112.2

Oracle's pick: Nation of Of Domination. Sawyer's turtle fortress looks imposing from a distance, but Chris brings Derrick Henry, Justin Jefferson and Tee Higgins. Sawyer answers with Bucky Irving, Malik Nabers and Brock Bowers, while Tyler Shough carries the hopes of the Saints faithful. This one is a coin flip; the Oracle sees the turtle shell cracking by a single cruel point.

We B. Tuten vs. Nice

Maggie 4–6 · Mike C 3–7 · ESPN 94.0–120.0

Oracle's pick: Nice. Maggie has apparently mistaken a fantasy lineup for long-term airport parking. Matthew Stafford and Jason Myers are on bye, leaving a quarterback-shaped hole in the cockpit. Mike C's Trevor Lawrence, CeeDee Lamb and Chase Brown provide a far sturdier flight plan. If Maggie replaces the bye-week starters, the projection could change dramatically.

PeePee Magee vs. Demon Worship

Jeff 4–6 · Jacob 7–3 · ESPN 88.7–114.5

Oracle's pick: Demon Worship. Jacob's forbidden altar hums with Saquon Barkley, James Cook, Zay Flowers and George Kittle. Jeff's crew, featuring Nico Collins and Omar Cooper Jr., could still produce surprises, but the 25.8-point projection gap is hard to ignore. From beneath the tarmac, the Michael Landry Goop bubbles approval at Jacob's deeply suspicious competence.

Shaving Ryans privates vs. Baba Yaga

Hayden 4–6 · Nathan 6–4 · ESPN 104.3–98.5

Oracle's pick: Shaving Ryans privates. The former champion Nathan faces a team whose name should never be announced over an airport intercom. Hayden has Dak Prescott, Christian McCaffrey and J.K. Dobbins; Nathan counters with Bo Nix, Jonathan Taylor and Garrett Wilson. Nathan even has Jadarian Price in a bye-week flex spot. The dethroned king may be defeated by basic roster management.

Oracle's Playoff Watch

Roy 9–1Sawyer 7–3Jacob 7–3Nathan 6–4

Roy remains the record-based front-runner, but Chris Arnold's upset opportunity is real. Sawyer and Jacob are the clearest pursuers; Nathan's 6–4 cushion is less comfortable than it looks. The 4–6 crowd can still create chaos, but nobody can afford a careless lineup now.
