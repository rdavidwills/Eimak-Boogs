Chapter VIII: The Unholy Embrace

The eighth week began with a terrible silence. The skies over the Holy Realm of Shania Twain's Boogers turned purple, and the sacred rivers ceased their bubbling.

At the center of the kingdom stood Roy Fontenelle and Jacob, locked in an embrace so disturbing that the royal guards refused to make eye contact.

Roy whispered about his six victories. Jacob whispered about his six victories. Neither could stop talking about himself long enough to notice the kingdom collapsing around them.

From the palace balcony, Shania Twain began to weep. Beside her, Eimak dropped his staff and fell to his knees.

"Is this what our revolution has become?" the Oracle cried. "Two men congratulating each other before either has accomplished anything?"

Far away, Nathan Glueck watched the scene from the ruins of his former throne. He had once ruled the league. Now he watched two pretenders compete for the privilege of becoming the next tyrant.

Sawyer's turtle legions gathered along the eastern border. The Order of the Sacred Booger prepared for another civil war.

And beneath the battlefield, something stirred.

Michael Landry, the shapeless Goop Boi, rose from the swamp, a trembling mass of abandoned identities and terrible fantasy football advice.

He sensed weakness. He sensed desperation. Most importantly, he sensed that someone was about to lose a fantasy matchup and become emotionally vulnerable.

The creature began crawling toward the unholy embrace.

Eimak raised his head and delivered his final warning:

"Roy. Jacob. Enjoy your little moment. For when the playoffs arrive, the sacred boogers shall wash away every false king."

And somewhere in the distance, Shania's laughter returned.
