Chapter VIII: The Unholy Embrace

On the eighth Sunday, a silence fell over the Sacred Booger Marsh. There stood Roy Fontenelle, once a proud swimmer in the green deluge, now clutching Jacob Thomas as though the last lifeboat in the realm were made of demon worship and questionable personal boundaries.

Jacob’s violet robes shimmered beneath the diseased moon. “Brother,” he whispered, “we are both six and one.” Roy sniffled. “Then why does nobody respect my greatness?” Jacob had no answer. He simply hugged harder.

From the ruined balcony, Shania Twain began to cry. Eimak, Oracle of Team Names, tore his crown from his head and howled: “I BUILT A REVOLUTION, NOT A SUPPORT GROUP!” Their tears mixed with the ancient mucus, causing the marsh to rise another inch.

Far away, Nathan Glueck polished the crown he insisted still belonged to him. Sawyer’s turtle army crept closer to the throne. Chris Polk’s soldiers raised the banner of the Nation of Of Domination, still refusing to correct the extra word. And in the darkest corner of the swamp, Michael Landry’s Goop Boi absorbed the sound of Roy and Jacob’s sobbing, growing more powerful with every embarrassing embrace.

The realm’s scribes declared this a new schism: the Brotherhood of the Unholy Hug against the Order of the Sacred Booger. The only certainty was that when Roy and Jacob faced each other on the fantasy battlefield, one of these affectionate monsters would have to lose.

And Eimak whispered his prophecy: “A hug cannot save you from the waiver wire.”
