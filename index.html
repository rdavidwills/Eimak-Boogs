Chapter VI · The False King

Five Wins. Zero Rings. One Grave.

Across the Holy Realm, bells rang in celebration of a king who had never earned a crown. Roy, the self-appointed monarch of Serving Kuentz, paraded his 5–0 record through the streets as though five regular-season wins could somehow erase a decade of championship impotence.

Eimak watched from his slime-soaked throne. “Ten seasons,” he whispered. “Ten seasons of watching this poor bastard mistake a temporary hot streak for a personality.”

From the western swamp emerged the skinny dwarf king, gripping a monstrous ball-and-chain mace. At his side rode Shania Twain, sparkling beneath the green storm. Together they marched to the burial pit, where a crooked stone bore a single name: ROY.

Roy protested. He cited his record. He cited Josh Allen. He cited Jonathan Taylor. He cited every statistic except the one carved into the bones of the realm: ZERO CHAMPIONSHIPS.

“You are not a dynasty,” Eimak roared. “You are a five-week free trial of competence. Your trophy case is so fucking empty it has developed an echo. A decade of drafting, trading and talking shit, and your crowning achievement is being undefeated in the middle of October. Congratulations, you magnificent fraud. The league has seen more silverware in a gas-station bathroom.”

Shania drove her shovel into the dirt. The dwarf swung his mace into the earth, and the ground split open. From the heavens came a flood of sacred green boogers, swallowing Roy's victory parade, his imaginary crown, and the last remaining shreds of his credibility.

Beyond the flood, Nathan's royalists rallied around Baba Yaga. Jacob's demonic faction summoned Michael Landry, the Goop Boi, whose shapeless body floated above the battlefield like a sentient puddle of bad decisions. The revolution had split into rival factions. But for one glorious week, all banners pointed toward a common cause: bury the false king.

And the Oracle issued his prediction: Booger Eaters shall challenge the unbeaten streak. If Lamar rises and Bijan delivers, Roy's perfect record will finally join his championship dreams six feet under.
