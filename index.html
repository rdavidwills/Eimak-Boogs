THE ORACLE HAS SPOKEN AGAIN

Eimak has risen from the ashes of RyHorn's broken house, but resurrection was only the beginning. In the second week, the stone beneath Rivieris has begun to speak. Six new battles have appeared before the Oracle, six Week 1 losers arrive carrying the stink of failure, and somewhere beneath the throne of the reigning champion, something has started whispering. Welcome back to the PREDICKS OF THE HOLY REALM OF SHANIA TWAIN'S BOOGERS.

☠️ The Shame of Week 1

Before Eimak looked forward, he looked backward.

Six houses had already tasted defeat: We B. Tuten, Nation of Of Domination, Dr. Alligator, Shaving Ryans privates, Homeless Doink Wagglers, and Baba Yaga.

The Oracle considered showing mercy.

He then remembered this was fantasy football.

We B. Tuten discovered that apparently having Jalen Hurts and Amon-Ra does not prevent public humiliation. Nation of Of Domination spent Week 1 dominating approximately nothing. Dr. Alligator rolled onto its back and waited for someone to rub its belly. Shaving Ryans privates somehow found an activity even less dignified than its team name. And the Homeless Doink Wagglers proved that sometimes the doink waggles you.

But then there was Nathan.

The reigning champion.

The keeper of last year's crown.

The mighty Baba Yaga entered Week 1 expecting the realm to kneel... and instead emerged 0–1.

Five men lost in Week 1. Nathan somehow made losing look like a ceremonial event. The crown remains on his head, although after one week it already appears to be sliding dangerously close to his eyebrows.

Eimak laughed.

Then the laughter stopped.

Because something underneath Nathan's throne laughed back.

📜 The Commissioner's Chronicle — Chapter II

The first week ended, and beneath the Temple of Rivieris, Eimak waited.

He had returned expecting screams.

Instead, he heard laughter.

Six houses had fallen. Six managers had watched the final numbers harden upon their screens and discovered the ancient truth of fantasy football: you can spend an entire offseason preparing only to get your ass kicked by someone who forgot to change a kicker.

We B. Tuten crawled from the battlefield wounded. Nation of Of Domination discovered that a name is not a strategy. Dr. Alligator sank quietly into the swamp. Shaving Ryans privates left behind several hairs and most of its dignity. The Homeless Doink Wagglers wandered back into the wilderness, their doinks noticeably less waggly than before.

And then came Nathan.

The reigning champion returned to his throne carrying a Week 1 loss.

He sat down anyway.

Eimak watched from beneath the stone.

Nathan adjusted the crown.

It slipped slightly to the left.

He adjusted it again.

Somewhere deep beneath him, something whispered.

Zero...

Nathan froze.

The chamber became silent.

Then came another whisper.

...and one.

Eimak smiled.

But the Oracle had not returned merely to torment an increasingly nervous fantasy champion. There were larger forces moving through Rivieris.

He placed both hands upon the ancient stone and six visions tore through him.

He saw Caleb Williams surrounded by purple fire. He saw Josh Allen standing beneath a storm while lions circled Buffalo. He saw Justin Jefferson carrying a wounded Nation from the battlefield. He saw Christian McCaffrey running while razors fell from the sky. He saw turtles marching slowly over abandoned doinks.

And then came the sixth vision.

Nathan stood alone upon the championship platform.

The banners behind him were gone.

The throne beneath him was cracked.

Across the darkness stood a figure Eimak could not yet recognize.

No sword was visible.

No revolution had begun.

Not yet.

But for the first time, Eimak understood the whispers beneath Rivieris.

They were not praying to the champion.

They were counting his losses.

THE BOOGERS HAVE BEGUN TO WHISPER.
