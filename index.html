THE CHRONICLES OF EIMAK: CHECKMATE IN THE HOLY REALM

In the tenth week, the sky above the Holy Realm turned the color of old pepperoni. From a mountain of abandoned championship merchandise descended Eimak, no longer merely an oracle but a Dark Lord clad in black iron, his eyes burning like two unpaid league dues.

At his side stood Sawyer's turtle, armored and silent, carrying the sacred shield of the shell. Across the chessboard sat Roy's pizza creature, a sweaty triangle of arrogance, mozzarella, and a deeply unsettling belief that regular-season success was a hereditary title.

“I am eight and one,” declared the Pizza Man.

“And yet,” replied Eimak, “the trophy case remains tragically spacious.”

Shania Twain reclined behind the board, unimpressed. The turtle advanced a pawn. Roy demanded a recount. Somewhere in the distance, Nathan's old crown rolled down a staircase while Maggie chased it with a broom.

Deep below the kingdom, the Michael Landry Goop began to pulse. It whispered to Jacob of an ancient bargain: surrender one playoff seed and receive unlimited mysterious dampness. Jacob, naturally, asked for details.

Above them all, Eimak raised his gauntlet. “One win separates the Turtle and the Pizza. But one truth separates them from greatness: neither has earned the right to be this annoying.”

And thus the pieces moved. The throne waited. The Oracle predicked.
